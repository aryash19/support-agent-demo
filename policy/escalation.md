# Escalation rules

Escalate refunds over $100, partial refunds, ownership disputes, ambiguous account
changes, suspected abuse, and requests outside documented policy.

Explain what needs review and why. Until a local escalation tool is implemented,
record the need for review in the customer reply; do not claim a ticket was created
or a human was contacted. Never invent approvals.

If a tool fails, report the known result accurately. Do not retry a write blindly.
Verify persisted state before saying a refund or account change succeeded.
