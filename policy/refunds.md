# Refund policy

All amounts are USD. Orders store integer cents to avoid rounding errors.

A delivered order is eligible within 30 calendar days of its order date,
inclusive. Future-dated orders are ineligible. The customer must own the order.
An automatic refund may not exceed $100.00 or the original order amount.
Refunds above $100.00 require human approval; the agent must escalate and must
not split a refund to bypass the limit.

This demo supports one full refund per order. Partial refunds require escalation.
Already refunded, shipped, and cancelled orders must not be automatically refunded.
Record the reason and refund date, and change the order status in one transaction.
Explain a denial or escalation clearly without claiming an action was completed.
